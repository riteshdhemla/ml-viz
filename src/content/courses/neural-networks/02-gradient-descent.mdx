# Gradient Descent

Training a neural network means finding weights that minimize a **loss function** —
a number that measures how wrong the network's predictions are.

## The core idea

Imagine standing on a hilly landscape in fog. You can feel the slope under your feet.
The steepest downhill direction is the **negative gradient**. Take a small step that way.
Repeat until you're in a valley.

$$
w \leftarrow w - \eta \cdot \frac{\partial L}{\partial w}
$$

Here $\eta$ is the **learning rate** — how big each step is.

<Callout type="warning" title="Learning rate matters">
  Too large: you overshoot the minimum and diverge.
  Too small: training takes forever and may get stuck in shallow minima.
</Callout>
