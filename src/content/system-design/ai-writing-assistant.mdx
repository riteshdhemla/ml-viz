# Design an AI Writing Assistant

<SystemDesignMeta
  track="Generative AI System Design"
  company="Jasper / Notion AI"
  scale="long-form · brand-voice control · grounded · preference-tuned"
  difficulty="intermediate"
/>

A writing assistant (marketing copy, blog posts, emails, docs) is a
**controllable long-form text generation** product. The model is given — the
system-design work is **control** (tone, style, brand voice, format),
**grounding** (facts, no hallucination), **long-form coherence**, and tuning
quality from **human preference**. It reads through the ML loop's objective →
evaluation → feedback stages.

## 1. Clarify the problem

- **Goal:** help users produce high-quality long-form text in a **controlled**
  style/format, grounded in provided facts/sources, efficiently.
- **Control axes:** tone, brand voice, length, format/structure, audience — the
  product's differentiator over raw chat.
- **Grounding:** for factual content, cite sources / stay faithful to provided
  material — hallucination is a brand risk for users.
- **Scope:** the generation product (control + grounding + long-form + feedback).

<Callout type="tip" title="Interview signal">
The differentiator isn't the model — it's **controllability** (reliable tone/
brand/format) and **grounding**. Frame it as steering and constraining a strong
base model, and tuning it with preference data.
</Callout>

## 2. Requirements & constraints

| Dimension | Target | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Reliable tone/brand/format | Structured prompting, style conditioning, fine-tuning |
| Grounding | Faithful to sources; cite | RAG over user material; faithfulness checks |
| Long-form | Coherent multi-section docs | Outline-then-expand; section planning |
| Quality | Matches human preference | RLHF/DPO on preference data |

<ThinkFirst question="Any frontier model already writes fluent prose. So where does a writing product actually add value?">
In everything fluency doesn't cover: **reliable control** (this brand's voice,
this format, every time — not "usually"), **grounding** (claims tied to the
user's sources, because a hallucinated statistic in a customer's blog post is
*their* reputation), and **long-form structure** (a 2,000-word piece that
doesn't drift). The base model is a commodity; the control system is the
product.
</ThinkFirst>

## 3. GenAI problem framing

**Controllable, grounded long-form generation** on top of a base LLM. Control
comes from (a) **structured prompting** (system prompt + style exemplars), (b)
optional **fine-tuning** on a brand's corpus for voice, and (c) **decoding**
choices. Grounding comes from **RAG** over the user's sources. Long-form coherence
comes from **plan-then-write** (generate an outline, expand sections) rather than
one long unstructured generation.

*Pre-generative baseline?* Templates + human writers — inflexible or slow.
Generative writing adapts to any prompt while (ideally) staying on-brand.

## 4. Model & generation *(spine: objective)*

- **Base + light adaptation:** a strong instruction-tuned base; **LoRA fine-tune**
  on a brand's writing for consistent voice where prompting isn't enough.
- **Decoding:** moderate temperature/top-p for fluent-but-controlled prose; the
  objective balances helpfulness with adherence to the style constraints.
- **Structure:** outline → section drafts → revise, so a 2000-word piece stays
  coherent and on-topic.

<SamplingViz />

Decoding parameters are a control surface: lower temperature for factual/brand-
safe copy, higher for ideation — often exposed to the user as "creativity."

<ThinkFirst question="A customer uploads brand guidelines and source documents. Through what mechanisms do those actually shape the generated text?">
Three, in increasing strength: **retrieval** pulls the sources and guidelines
into context with a requirement to cite; **style conditioning** puts voice
exemplars and explicit constraints (tone, reading level, format) in the
prompt; and where prompting isn't reliable enough, a **LoRA adapter**
fine-tuned on the brand's corpus bakes the voice in. Format guarantees come
from constrained decoding, not hope.
</ThinkFirst>

## 5. Grounding & control

- **RAG:** retrieve the user's provided sources/brand guidelines into context;
  generate with citations; verify claims are supported.
- **Style conditioning:** brand-voice exemplars and explicit constraints (tone,
  reading level, format) in the prompt or a fine-tuned adapter.
- **Structured output:** enforce format (sections, bullet limits, JSON for
  structured docs) via constrained decoding / schemas.

<WikiLink slug="text-generation-metrics" title="Text-generation quality metrics" />

<ThinkFirst question="Fluency is table stakes — every draft reads well. What do you actually measure to know the product is working?">
The things fluency hides: **control adherence** (did it match the requested
tone/length/format — a checkable constraint), **faithfulness** (is every claim
supported by the provided sources), and the bluntest product signal of all —
**edit rate**: how much of the draft users rewrite before shipping it. A
beautiful draft that gets 80% rewritten failed.
</ThinkFirst>

## 6. Evaluation *(spine: evaluation)*

- **Human preference / LLM-as-judge:** the primary signal for quality, tone
  adherence, and helpfulness; track win-rate vs. the previous version.
- **Faithfulness:** when grounded, are claims supported by the sources? (judge +
  human audit).
- **Control adherence:** does output match requested tone/length/format? — a
  measurable constraint check.
- **Product metrics:** acceptance/edit rate (how much users change the draft).

<WikiLink slug="llm-as-judge" title="LLM-as-a-judge for writing quality" />

## 7. Feedback & operations *(spine: feedback)*

- **Preference loop:** user edits, regenerations, and thumbs feed **RLHF/DPO** and
  prompt improvements — the model gets better at *this product's* notion of good
  writing.
- **Serving:** streaming for responsiveness; model tiering (draft vs. polish);
  caching of retrieved brand material.
- **Guardrails:** plagiarism/verbatim-source checks, disallowed-content filters,
  and disclosure that content is AI-assisted.

## 8. Tradeoffs & alternatives

| Choice | Alternative | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plan-then-write | One long generation | Long-form coherence and structure control |
| Prompt + LoRA for voice | Full fine-tune | Cheap, adapter-per-brand, easy to update |
| RAG grounding | Rely on parametric knowledge | Faithfulness, freshness, citeability |
| Preference (RLHF/DPO) tuning | Prompt-only | Aligns to the product's quality bar over time |

## 9. Interviewer follow-ups

<Details summary="Output is fluent but off-brand. How do you enforce voice?">
Layer control: brand-voice exemplars and explicit style constraints in the prompt
first; if that's insufficient, LoRA fine-tune on the brand's corpus for a
consistent voice adapter. Measure tone/voice adherence explicitly (rubric-based
LLM-judge + human) so 'off-brand' is caught pre-ship, not by users.
</Details>

<Details summary="The assistant invents statistics in a factual article. Fix?">
Ground it: retrieve the user's sources and require claims to be supported +
cited, run a faithfulness check (judge over claim vs. source), and for
unsupported claims, hedge or omit rather than fabricate. Ungrounded long-form is
a hallucination magnet — RAG + a faithfulness gate is the standard remedy.
</Details>

<Details summary="How do you keep a 2000-word piece coherent and on-topic?">
Plan-then-write: generate an outline, expand each section conditioned on the
outline + prior sections, then a revision pass for consistency. One monolithic
generation drifts and repeats; explicit structure keeps long-form on track and
lets the user steer at the outline level.
</Details>

## Key takeaways

- A writing assistant is **controllable, grounded long-form generation** — the
  value is in **control (tone/brand/format)** and **faithfulness**, not the base
  model.
- Use **plan-then-write** for coherence, **RAG** for grounding, and **prompt +
  LoRA** for brand voice.
- Tune quality with **human-preference (RLHF/DPO)** and measure **control adherence
  + faithfulness**, not just fluency.
